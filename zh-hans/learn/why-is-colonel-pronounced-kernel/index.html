COLONEL 为什么读成 "kernel"？！英语发音大坑

Alex和Sofia，不到一分钟讲清楚。

为什么会这样

拼写是 colonel，读音却是 "KER-nel", , 跟爆米花里的玉米粒 kernel 一个音。里面明明连个 R 都没有。

这个词没有任何规则能救你，只能硬背：KER-nel，两拍。

教练说了什么

照视频里听到的原样，用英文写出来。

AlexColonel. C-O-L-O-N-E-L. And it's pronounced… 'KER-nel'. Are you having me on?

SofiaYep. Like the popcorn kernel. KER-nel.

AlexThere's no R in that word. Where did the R come from?!

SofiaNobody knows. English borrowed it from two languages and panicked. Just memorise this one.

AlexTwo beats, hard 'ker' at the front. And yes, it makes no sense. Welcome to English.
