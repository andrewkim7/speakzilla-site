Why natives say GONNA (and you should too)

With Alex and Sofia, in under a minute.

What's going on

Gonna, wanna, gotta aren't lazy, they're natural English.

Function words flatten in real speech: "to" → "tuh", "and" → "n", "going to" → "gonna". Saying every word fully is the textbook-learner tell.

What the coaches say

AlexGonna. Wanna. Gotta. These aren't lazy English, they're the real thing.

SofiaNatives say them without even thinking. It's just how the language sounds.

AlexThe small words flatten. 'Going to' becomes gonna. 'Want to' becomes wanna.

Sofia'To' turns into 'tuh'. 'And' becomes barely a hum, fish 'n' chips.

AlexSay every word in full and you sound like a textbook. Reduce them, and you sound native.
